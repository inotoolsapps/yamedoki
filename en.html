Start free trials without worry. Stop on the right day.

Yamedoki is an Android app that reminds you before free trials end and before subscriptions renew. It also finds the ones you no longer use. Your data never leaves your phone.

Get it on Google Play (coming soon) See how it works

Android 8.0 or later. Free to use.

What it does

Add the name, the price and the date. Yamedoki remembers the date and reminds you.

How it works

  1. Add a subscriptionPick from subscription apps on your phone, or type it in. Share the text of a signup email and Yamedoki reads the price and date.
  2. Then just waitYamedoki checks your dates once a day at the time you choose and only reminds you when it matters.
  3. Keep it or cancel itOpen the cancellation page from the reminder, or tap “I'll keep it” to skip that reminder.

Your data stays on your phone

Yamedoki doesn't have permission to access the internet. Nothing you add is ever sent anywhere.

No account

No email address, no name. Install it and start.

No bank or card linking

Yamedoki never handles account or card details. You type prices in, or it reads them from text you share.

Usage is dates only

Showing when you last used an app is optional. Yamedoki reads only when each app was last used, never what's inside it.

See the privacy policy for details.

About the free-trial catalog

The app includes a list of services with free trials. Before you sign up, you can check the trial length, the price afterwards and how to cancel. Sign up on the official site, come back to the app, and Yamedoki saves the end date for you.

Some catalog links will be ads (affiliate links). They're marked “PR”, and Yamedoki may earn a referral fee if you sign up through them. Reminders and the unused-subscription check work the same whether or not a link is an ad.

Questions

Can I cancel from the app?

No. Yamedoki opens the cancellation page for you, and you finish cancelling on that page.

Which countries does it support?

Adding subscriptions and reminders work in any country. The free-trial catalog covers Japan, the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Germany, France and South Korea. The app is available in 9 languages: English, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Korean, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese.

What happens when I change phones?

Your data is stored only on your phone, so for now it doesn't move to a new one. A way to transfer it is planned.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Yamedoki is Android only for now.
