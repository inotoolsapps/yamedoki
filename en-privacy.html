Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android app Yamedoki (“the app”) handles information.

1. Information we collect

The developer of the app does not collect personal information. The app has no permission to access the internet and never sends what you enter or information about your device anywhere. There is no account.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device so the app can work. No one outside your device, including the developer, can see it.

  • The subscriptions you add: names, prices, currencies, dates, cancellation page URLs and similar details
  • Settings such as the reminder time and the catalog region

This information is deleted when you uninstall the app. Device backup is turned off for the app.

3. Permissions and why they're used

PermissionPurpose
NotificationsTo remind you before free trials end and before renewals
Run at startupTo keep reminders scheduled after the phone restarts
Usage access (optional)To show when you last used the app of each subscription you added. Used only if you allow it in settings. The app reads only the last time each app was used, never its content or what you did in it
Checking whether specific apps are installedTo suggest common subscription apps in “Choose from apps on this phone”. Only the apps on the list built into Yamedoki are checked

4. Links to other sites

The app may open services' official pages, cancellation pages and Google Play subscriptions in your browser or other apps. Some links in the free-trial catalog are affiliate links (ads) and are marked “PR”. On those sites, the site or the advertising company may handle information using cookies or similar technology, under that site's own privacy policy.

5. Reading shared text

When you share text to the app from another app, it reads the service name, price and date from that text and fills them in. This happens only on your device; the text is not saved or sent anywhere.

6. Contact and feedback

If you email the developer with a question or feedback, your email address and message are used only to reply and to improve the app. They are not shared with third parties except where required by law.

7. Children

The app does not collect personal information, including from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
